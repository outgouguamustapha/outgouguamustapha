## Featured Projects
 
### 1. [Sales Performance & Profitability Analytics](https://github.com/outgouguamustapha/sales-analysis-dashboard)
 
**Domain:** Commercial Finance & Retail  
**Tools:** `Power BI` `DAX` `Data Modeling`
 
> Executive analytics suite diagnosing revenue velocity, unit economics, and profit margin health across products and regions.
 
- **Highlights:** DAX measures for Month-to-Date (MTD) revenue, Month-over-Month (MoM) growth, and rep quota tracking
- **Impact:** Pinpointed margin dilution in high-volume categories caused by excessive discounting, with actionable pricing recommendations
- **Live report:** [Launch dashboard →](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiMjBmYTFjYWItODI3Yy00YWM2LTljNjItYmNjNjliNzA5MGY4IiwidCI6Ijg0ZDI3MGQyLTBiYzUtNGQ1NS1iZjBhLTI3NGYxYTU3NmNiZiJ9)

### 2. [HR Recruitment & Talent Acquisition Analytics](https://github.com/outgouguamustapha/hr-hiring-analytics-dashboard)
 
**Domain:** People Analytics  
**Tools:** `Power BI` `DAX` `Funnel Analysis`
 
> Diagnostic HR dashboard tracking candidate pipeline progression, drop-off rates, and recruiter capacity.
 
- **Highlights:** Multi-stage conversion funnel (*Application → Screening → Interviews → Offer → Hired*) with dynamic pacing indicators
- **Impact:** Isolated mid-funnel technical screening bottlenecks and matched offer acceptance rates to compensation expectations
---
 
### 3. [Enterprise SQL Data Warehouse & Customer 360 Analytics](https://github.com/outgouguamustapha/sql-data-warehouse-project)
 
**Domain:** Customer Analytics & Business Intelligence  
**Tools:** `T-SQL` `SQL Server` `Star Schema` `Advanced Analytics`
 
> Data warehouse consolidating ERP and CRM data to analyze customer purchasing patterns, revenue concentration, and product performance.
 
- **Highlights:** Customer segmentation (VIP vs. Regular tiers), rolling 3-month moving averages, and cumulative revenue rankings
- **Impact:** Unified Customer 360 views covering lifetime value (LTV), recency, order frequency, and Pareto revenue distribution
