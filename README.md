## Featured Analytics Projects

### 1. [Sales Performance & Profitability Analytics](https://github.com/outgouguamustapha/sales-analysis-dashboard)
> **Domain:** Commercial Finance & Retail Analytics  
> **Tools:** Microsoft Power BI, DAX, Data Modeling, PRD  
> Built an executive analytics suite to diagnose revenue velocity, unit economics, and profit margin health across commercial products and regions.
* **Analytical Highlights:** Formulated DAX calculations for Month-to-Date (MTD) revenue, Month-over-Month (MoM) growth rates, and rep quota tracking.
* **Business Impact:** Pinpointed margin dilution in high-volume product categories caused by excessive discounting, delivering actionable pricing recommendations.
* **Live Interactive Report:** [Launch Live Dashboard](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiMjBmYTFjYWItODI3Yy00YWM2LTljNjItYmNjNjliNzA5MGY4IiwidCI6Ijg0ZDI3MGQyLTBiYzUtNGQ1NS1iZjBhLTI3NGYxYTU3NmNiZiJ9)

### 2. [HR Recruitment & Talent Acquisition Analytics](https://github.com/outgouguamustapha/hr-hiring-analytics-dashboard)
> **Domain:** People Analytics & Talent Acquisition  
> **Tools:** Microsoft Power BI, DAX, Funnel Analysis, Process Optimization  
> Developed a diagnostic HR dashboard tracking candidate pipeline progression, drop-off rates, and recruiter capacity.
* **Analytical Highlights:** Structured multi-stage conversion funnels (*Application &rarr; Screening &rarr; Interviews &rarr; Offer &rarr; Hired*) with dynamic pacing indicators.
* **Business Impact:** Isolated mid-funnel technical screening bottlenecks, evaluated hiring rates, and matched offer acceptance rates to compensation expectations.

---

### 3. [Commercial Sales & Customer 360 Analytics](https://github.com/outgouguamustapha/sql-data-warehouse-project)
> **Domain:** Customer Analytics & Business Intelligence  
> **Tools:** T-SQL, Star Schema Modeling, Exploratory & Advanced Analytics  
> Analyzed customer purchasing behaviors, revenue concentrations, and product performance using complex SQL models.
* **Analytical Highlights:** Created customer segmentation models (VIP vs. Regular tiers), rolling 3-month moving averages, and cumulative revenue rankings.
* **Business Impact:** Delivered Customer 360 views highlighting lifetime value (LTV), recency, order frequency, and Pareto revenue distributions.

---

### 4. [Enterprise SQL Data Warehouse & Commercial Analytics](https://github.com/outgouguamustapha/sql-data-warehouse-project)
> **Domain:** Customer Analytics & Business Intelligence  
> **Tools:** T-SQL, SQL Server, Star Schema Modeling, Exploratory & Advanced Analytics 
> Engineered a comprehensive data warehouse consolidating ERP and CRM data to analyze customer purchasing patterns, revenue concentration, and product performance.
* **Analytical Highlights:** Created customer segmentation models (VIP vs. Regular tiers), rolling 3-month moving averages, and cumulative revenue rankings.
* **Business Impact:** Delivered unified Customer 360 views highlighting customer lifetime value (LTV), recency, order frequency, and Pareto revenue distributions.
